Teacher Guide & Curriculum Mapping

This interactive arcade allows students to formulate qualitative likelihood predictions prior to engaging in physical/simulated trial games.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Assign qualitative values (Impossible to Certain) to events.
  • Compare initial predictions with experimental frequencies.
  • Understand how sample sizes (trial counts) affect experimental outcomes.

Classroom Instructions

  1. Pick a booth and read the Situation Statement.
  2. Select a Qualitative Prediction from the 5 scale buttons.
  3. Play the interactive game to execute required trial attempts.
  4. Review your certificate and analytical table!
